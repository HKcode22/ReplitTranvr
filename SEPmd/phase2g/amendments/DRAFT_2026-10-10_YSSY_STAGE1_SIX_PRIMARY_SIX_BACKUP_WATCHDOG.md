## 1. Scientific judgment of the proposal

**Changing supervisor health-check failure tolerance from 3 to 6 primary + 6 backup (12 total) does not *in itself* alter flight feature definitions, source UTC, classification labels or physical-flight-v2 identity. It nevertheless is not scientifically neutral.** It changes stopping/censoring/paid exposure and may change which airport/time windows produce accepted samples. Outage probability can correlate with notifications, flight traffic and hosting load (informative missingness). A longer threshold without independent ingress can *increase* undetected missing paid data and selective acceptance. It is invalid to retroactively reinterpret P2G22/23/24 outcomes or claim a scientific PASS based on return of a health response.

**WSSS prior evidence:** two consecutive 15-second health monitor failures then recovered before #3; WSSS scientific/accounting result subsequently independently adjudicated. This supports the assertion that isolated monitor glitches need not invalidate science, not that a third failure would necessarily lose real flight data. **YSSY P2G24** had three failures then the owner terminated; there is no independently observed fourth sample nor validated provider source preservation.

**Decision:** 6+6 is a worthwhile *separate experimental candidate*, not demonstrably superior to 6+4 on present outage traces. The two extra conditional checks create the possibility of rescuing a recovery on health checks #11 or #12, but also delay safety shutdown under uncertainty. We must collect objective failure-duration, backlog and billable-loss metrics to select a threshold.

## 2. Exact distinction between checks and provider retries

- The actual GitHub-owned **supervisor** in `scripts/v39_phase2g_stage1_logged_supervisor_v39.ts` currently polls every **15,000 ms** and terminates after **3 consecutive failed four-stage callback-health cycles**; its four stages are wrong-secret route, deployed source/runtime, actual secret binding, and runtime DB binding. An internal subcheck can time out after 8 seconds; poll overlap is skipped. **Twelve consecutive cycles is NOT guaranteed to represent exactly 180 seconds** of outage; record real wall clock and impose an independently reviewed maximum elapsed-downtime bound in the future live implementation.
- Proposed **6 PRIMARY consecutive failed cycles, then up to 6 additional BACKUP** conditional failed cycles. Healthy response resets the counter but **does not clear unresolved external-provider/source/billing/scientific audits**.
- The independent AeroDataBox **paid webhook-delivery retry contract remains `maxDeliveryRetries=0`**. The number twelve is *not* a setting sent to AeroDataBox, and no automatic extra provider API calls are authorized.

## 3. Evidence-gated, continuously re-evaluated decision

1. **Healthy complete callback binding:** monitoring can continue; science PASS remains separate.
2. **Recoverable 502/503/504, timeout or transport failure:** conditional 6 PRIMARY checks allowed only with continuously authenticated independent sender-attempt watermark; exact external attempted items/credits matched to immutable edge receipts; verified original wire SHA + first-edge UTC, full raw readback and 168-hour retention; all *elapsed* 15min scientific bins and physical-v2 items verifiable, future bins not fabricated; unique owner/subscription, signed source and within frozen bounded credit budget; Queue live with nonexpired backlog; valid UNLOGGED recovery provenance.
3. **Failures #7–#11:** may use up to 5 of 6 backup opportunities *with the same evidence refreshed at every check*. Example: eleven consecutive unhealthy checks followed by check #12 healthy **could** continue monitoring in a fully authenticated source-preserving system. This is explicitly not yet a real production capability.
4. **Failure #12:** bounded supervisor termination using already-approved settlement/cleanup/failover path; do not create a second subscription or silently recreate a paid session. Terminal *scientific* adjudication is separate: whether all frozen 120 minutes / eight bins, billed attempts and original physical identities survived is an empirical question.
5. **Immediate hard stop at ANY check including healthy 200:** wrong secret/source revision/DB identity, invalid route contract, owner split brain/overlapping provider subscription, known unmatched billable attempt (e.g. 260 vs 259), known lost original bytes/UTC/physical-v2 item, expired queue or frozen budget ceiling breach. Ambiguous signed sender evidence/stale watermark during outage disallows extension from its first unverified check.
6. **Positive outcome and reporting:** record each monitor sample, actual elapsed downtime, longest consecutive failed check run, first and last source-UTC, original immutable evidence completeness and replay delay, externally reconciled credits, all 8x15m bins, confirmed distinct physical-v2 flights and any censored/missing periods. Preserve every historical FAILED/CENSORED result.
