## Safety/scientific boundaries

- The independent sender ledger is signed by a **test fixture**, *not AeroDataBox*. It cannot certify real provider origin, exact billing, signatures or retries.
- The edge HMAC proves only that the experimental edge made the signed observation; it does **not** authenticate the original upstream provider. No production signed provider wire contract was verified.
- The sender frame currently references an opaque prospective owner freeze SHA supplied by the caller, **not a separately validated owner signature**. Do not convert a caller-supplied `independentOwnerSessionBindingVerified` Boolean into a release proof.
- Internal matches are constructed from declared rows or, in one test, actual SQL receipt SHA plus object readback. The current V3.9 internal schema does not independently retain authenticated original edge receipt UTC/attempt HMAC. A matching count is not reconstruction.
- No source-side 120-minute **real wall-clock** hosted receiver availability; no actual R2/Queue/networked storage performance; no real published Autoscale/restart simulation. No Queue/SQL atomic recovery, 24h vs 168h raw evidence fix, owner/watchdog signed recovery protocol, post-crash physical-flight-v2/bin completeness guarantee or lawful retention permission.
- Historical P2G22 260/259, P2G23/P2G24 censored status, original raw hashes/source bytes and frozen F.8 treatment remain unchanged. Quinn's Replit support ticket #564568 original P2G24 instance-termination cause is still awaiting confirmation.
