## Minimal scientific and mathematical controls
1. **Define estimand:** provider-observable departures from named operational population; distinguish descriptive research on received YSSY-B from original five-anchor confirmatory sample. Pre-register final sampling redesign before any affected main-run dispatch; no silently biased regional extrapolation.
2. **Define as-of:** for each snapshot, `available_at <= prediction_cutoff`; T selected from actual provider-native schedule with frozen semantics; target labels actual vs estimated distinguished; no retrospective horizon backfill. Immutable flight identity, codeshare de-dupe, censor/unknown labels and chrono/group holdout.
3. **Record missingness:** per airport/tier/region/flight/target and billed/observed credits; source SHA if held legally. `229/231` is a billed-unit reconciliation fraction, **NOT** iid webhook-delivery probability. Diagnose possible MNAR/delay-biased missingness, compare exclusion/sensitivity for exploratory ML. NIST RDaF, STROBE guidelines do not impose universal 0.5%, 1%, 99% success thresholds.
4. **Exact financial safety:** credit headroom/reserve/cap and owned subscriptions are quantitative controls that cannot be relaxed because an ML model tolerates missingness. Gate3 exact tol=0, stop on future unexplained accounting gaps under frozen Plan unless an explicit prospective change; preserve research-only observations even on operational failure.
5. **31-day budget arithmetic:** Phase6 calendar is 31 parent batch days with daily ceiling 1900, not a required daily 1900 burn. 31×1900=58,900 > project's MAX_DESIGN_CEILING 57,900; run-total, protected floor, pre-run spend, account API-unit quota and seven-category REST budget further constrain legal spending. Never promise 31×1900 spend or 1900 labels/day.
