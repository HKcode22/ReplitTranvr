## Critical sampling observation: FOUR ANCHORS DO NOT EQUAL FOUR AIRPORTS PER STUDY
Preserve original Plan §8: **each sampled Phase6 batch includes 1 anchor HUB, 2 independently frame-eligible MID, and 1 REGIONAL airport**, with frozen tier×region balancing, route/tail identity, timezone/UTC six-slot schedule and genuine PRE/POST feed eligibility. The four-anchor pool only controls the ONE HUB slot. Independent MID/REGIONAL coverage is what expands geographic and route reach beyond Singapore/Abu Dhabi/Bogotá/Sydney; do not promise complete world-airport/route observation.

Each new HUB draw rotates among four equally eligible declared sites with no repeat until all four have appeared. A crossover period-2 replay is exempt as original Plan requires. **Five matched crossover PAIRS remain five window treatment contrasts, NOT five independent anchors**; one HUB can occur in more than one pair. Inference standard errors and effect generalization must account for repeated anchor/site clusters, geography, nonrandom site selection and correlations. With four hub groups, avoid asymptotic global-airport inference.
