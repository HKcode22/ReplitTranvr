## 1. Prospective study target: what we can actually measure

**Primary collection estimand, revised:** for a scheduled provider-observable flight that is in the explicitly frozen source-supported airport/service/time frame, estimate (i) **provider-reported cancellation/diversion** and (ii) **provider-reported schedule revision of ≥15 minutes** as of a predeclared post-service observation. These are two separately scored targets, NEVER collapsed into real aircraft wheels-off delay, actual departure, airline-reported final truth or a joint negative inferred from no webhook. This is a *provider-observable operational disruption* study; record the original runway-time objective as **unavailable/secondary**, not a blocking condition or a false success.

- **Prediction schedule reference `S`:** the earliest *actually recorded/available* AeroDataBox `departure.scheduledTime.utc` in a role-aware request-primary FIDS response. Name it `provider_reported_S`; do not claim gate-out, scheduled wheels-off or official airline schedule. Use prospective S−24h, S−6h, S−90m cutoffs; if the schedule was not observed before cutoff, that horizon is **INELIGIBLE_UNOBSERVED**, not later backfilled.
- **Status target:** `CANCELED_REPORTED`, `DIVERTED_REPORTED`, `OTHER_TERMINAL_REPORTED`, `PENDING`, `CONFLICTING`, `UNKNOWN` kept separate. Reported cancellation/diversion is measured only from a positively identified post-service original. A negative requires an affirmative, appropriately finalized non-canceled/non-diverted **terminal** status from a post-service source. Silence, not-found and loss of webhooks are not negatives.
- **Schedule revision target:** measure difference between first observed `S` and **later observed** provider schedule/revision field under a frozen version/clock rule, threshold 15 minutes absolute change for the `SCHEDULE_REVISION_15M` target. A source's `revisedTime` may be a predicted flight movement, not a revision of `scheduledTime`; therefore **only comparable provider-reported schedule versions count**. If unavailable, mark `CENSORED_UNOBSERVED_VERSION`. This outcome predicts **change in provider report**, not actual delay. No observed second version is not proof of no change; report a measured 0 only with affirmative, sufficient source evidence.
- **Secondary** available ADSB.lol aircraft position/ground-state traces (with verified physical-leg match, ODbL conditions and observed-time availability), optional 15-minute to hours **takeoff interval proxy**. Do not call interval estimate actual wheels-off. For delayed US-domestic cross-checks, BTS `WheelsOff` and `CRSDepTime` are distinct, free but delayed. Neither is required for worldwide month-long primary S/operational-status collection.
- **Free supporting weather**: NOAA AviationWeather.gov METAR/TAF with receipt and forecast-issue clocks; missing weather must not suppress an eligible PRE.
