## 4. Free-source supplementation — exact evidence classes

| Source | What can be obtained without a new paid aviation subscription | Can it fill original f.8 target? | Hard limitations |
|---|---|---|---|
| [BTS reporting-carrier on-time](https://www.transtats.bts.gov/Fields.asp?gnoyr_VQ=FGJ) | Published U.S. **reporting** carrier domestic flight row: actual `WheelsOff`, `DepTime` (gate departure), `CRSDepTime` and `TaxiOut`, with historical airline/flight/date/airport | **Actual-only wheels-off reference on an exactly linked US reporting-flight subcohort, after publication**, useful for retrospective validation. **CRSDepTime is scheduled gate departure in the common on-time table; do not conflate with scheduled wheels-off**. | Does not apply to WSSS/YSSY or nonreported worldwide flights; published with months' lag (as of Nov 2 2026 source lists latest Aug 2026); actual postflight label cannot be an as-of PRE feature |
| [ADSB.lol open historical daily](https://www.adsb.lol/docs/open-data/historical) | Globally collected aircraft position/altitude/ground-state traces, often tail/callsign; ODbL | **Independent inferred takeoff interval proxy** after high-quality aircraft/flight/time/place match; helps missingness/cross-source observability, **not exact certified runway time** | Incomplete receiver coverage, aircraft identity ambiguity, schedule/cancellation not represented, attribution/share-alike obligations |
| [NOAA AviationWeather API](https://aviationweather.gov/data/api/) | Worldwide METAR/TAF and limited recent history, rate-limited | **Optional as-of weather features/stratification only**, not flight schedule/actual landing/takeoff | Must record forecast issued/received clock, not use future weather at PRE |
| [OpenSky](https://opensky-network.org/data) | Historical position datasets and possible free research access for qualified university researchers | **Secondary ADS-B/trajectory crosscheck**, no global airline schedule or certified actual-only runway | Large historical access requires eligibility/approval; [automated operational REST needs prior written agreement](https://opensky-network.org/about/terms-of-use), even where monetary price is zero |
| [OurAirports public data](https://ourairports.com/data/) | Airport identifiers, regions, coordinates; public-domain | Airport-frame/identifier crosswalk, **not flight population or actual movement** | Community-maintained and not authoritative source of flight-specific operational truth |

**Decision:** Keep ADB FIDS as the provider-observable schedule/population. ADSB.lol + NOAA can complement it and describe missingness; BTS is a high-quality delayed U.S. actual-only reference on a correctly matched subset. *No verified universally free source substitutes for a worldwide independent predeparture airline schedule + exact actual runway event in the month-long operational window.* Do not treat OpenSky academic access as automatically provisioned or free to use in a live system.
