## D7. Technical study note: why four HUBs and five pairs are compatible

A **HUB anchor** is one airport occupying the single HUB slot in a 4-airport batch. A **crossover pair** is two parent experimental batches with the *same four airport identities* but different service exposure shapes. Therefore **4 unique HUB identities ≠ 5 crossover pairs**. One of the four anchor identities may appear in more than one pair, with repeated-airport covariance addressed in analysis.

`effectiveCarriers` above is a **numeric diversity index**, not a literal airline count (hence its fractional values). `intlShare` is between 0 and 1 and describes historical reference movements, not AeroDataBox's prospective route capture rate. A database `frame_hash` validates the versioned source-reference mapping, while `pre_eligible` and `post_eligible` are **frame flags**, not proof that every T−24h original arrived or that an actual physical departure occurred.
