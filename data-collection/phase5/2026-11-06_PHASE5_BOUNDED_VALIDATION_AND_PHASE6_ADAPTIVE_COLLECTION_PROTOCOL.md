## 3. Research design decision — yes to controlled improvement; no to a changing unknown dataset

**Scientific sources**:
- [STROBE 2007](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2636253/): predefine variables/measurements and report each stage's eligible/population/retained/analysed denominators, missing values, biases and sensitivity analyses.
- [TRIPOD+AI 2024](https://www.bmj.com/content/385/bmj-2023-078378): transparency about training/evaluation data, cohort selection and outcome/missingness, carefully qualified model generalisability. Developed for clinical predictive studies but principles transfer to data-provenance and ML reporting; it does not certify this aviation model.
- [ICH M14 real-world study QA (2026)](https://www.fda.gov/media/179795/download): document data accrual and cleaning audit trails and modifications. This is methodological guidance, not an aviation regulatory requirement.
- [AeroDataBox Flight Alert guide 2026](https://aerodatabox.com/flight-alert-api-2026/): subscription CREATE is separate and free in credit-based system, but **every notification item SEND** (including failed delivery) costs credits; retries also cost; high-traffic airport subscriptions can drain balance. Never claim a fixed provider-side spend cap where none is guaranteed.
- [AeroDataBox Sep7 terms notice](https://aerodatabox.com/2026-09-terms-update): revised legal/retention terms generally effective for existing subscribers **Nov7, 2026**, subject to actual account plan/exceptions. Paid authorization is NOT provider content retention / ML training permission.
- [ADSB.lol](https://api.adsb.lol/docs): ODbL data, operator asks coordination before production use. Research source ≠ automatically authorized month-long continuous service.

**Core frozen before Phase6:** dataset frame/sampling strata/weights, FIDS source/service/role inclusion, prospective S+three cutoff definitions, positive operating-leg matching threshold, PRE feature/availability clock rules, fixed target and label taxonomy, the primary training/evaluation split unit, project API and separate Alert budget, source-use/retention constraints and independent audit. Collect flights irrespective of eventual webhook/delay. Any unsupported outcome is UNKNOWN/CENSORED/NOT_APPLICABLE (do not label no webhook as no delay).

**Allowed operational learning daily:** status checks, configuration repairs, retry behavior within approved budget, annotation, low-risk instrument logging. If collection code/identity source/label availability changes, bump `protocol_version` and `collector_build_sha`; record exact effective UTC (no retroactive PRE/backfill), affected airports/horizons and raw/normalized/label coverage by version; do not silently mix days. An outage is measured missingness only when the denominator and detection interval are known. If no source available for an entire target, suspend that source/target and use a separately preregistered alternate head; cannot call it ordinary 3-minute gap.

**Daily minimum ledger** per source/horizon/airport/tier/UTC: requests planned/attempted/reserved/succeeded; unique operator-confirmed vs conditional/marketing; `PRE_expected` and `PRE_persisted_on_time`; Alerts subscription created vs provider SEND credits vs acknowledged valid raw original vs identity inside/outside/unresolved vs missing; AIRBORNE eligible/seen/linked/usable; POST comparable outcome/positive/negative/censored; fail-to-write, disconnect duration, rights expiry, authorized unit ceiling. Unknown denominator -> `NULL`, not zero. Estimate pooled measures only on comparable version/protocol strata. Split training and evaluation by *physical operating leg* and time; do not leak near-duplicate horizon rows or post-service labels into PRE features. A late, prospective holdout can assess distribution drift.
