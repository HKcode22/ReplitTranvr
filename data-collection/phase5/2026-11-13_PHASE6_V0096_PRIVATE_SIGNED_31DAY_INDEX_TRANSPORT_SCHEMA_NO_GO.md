### Critical research-continuity limitation to solve, not conceal

The existing full **cumulative** index validation requires the prior revision's actual bytes for every append. If a legal owner purge removes historical private revision bytes after 7 days, a naive cumulative 31-day chain could become impossible to extend or revalidate. In addition, a 31-day cumulative index could carry provider-derived identifiers older than their allowed retention period. **V0096 does not solve that rights/data-minimization question.** Before full 31-day GO we need either (a) authentic 31-day Contents/derived-index rights and compliant backup retention or (b) a separately reviewed nonreconstructive digests/metadata chain with bounded live current revisions and no leaked historical flight Contents.
