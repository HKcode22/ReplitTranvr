The actual precommitted five-pair sampling pool was 1,013 B0062 strict MID/REGIONAL reference records (427 MID + 586 REGIONAL) and the four owner-selected HUBs WSSS/OMAA/SKBO/YSSY. Its selected-slot SHA-256 **f5112a9ca432d9d3fede96a9805030d68e05ced5a19d029aa896805746c21592** was independently recomputed; exactly pair3 MID_A KTEB→KBUF changed and the other 19 slot identities remain identical. This is now **complete frame/sample evidence**, but it still does **NOT** certify future live provider scheduled-flight reachability or a signed paired calendar.

Reference frame feed flags and annual observed commercial-movement proxies alone are insufficient to demonstrate airline passenger departures on particular future run dates. For experimental comparability, screen **all** assigned crossover airports under the same **pre-outcome, date-specific** rule, not only airports flagged by an analyst after looking at attractive results. An intermittent scheduled service is not the same as a regulatory no-service restriction; there must not be opportunistic substitutions merely to make data yield look high.

**Critical distinctions:** “A timetable lists some scheduled services” is not “AeroDataBox provider returns those flights with usable source and actual labels.” “No scheduled service in this particular 14-day interval” is not “this airport is permanently prohibited from scheduled service.” “A regular scheduled flight exists” is not “we have enough independent routes and disruptions to accurately predict.” Marketing codeshares must collapse to physical operating legs; otherwise apparent route diversity and sample size are inflated.

## Consequence for modeling and routes

This preliminary screen already illustrates substantial heterogeneity: a multiple-route MID (reported KBUF), a one-destination REGIONAL (KDEC), a currently zero-departure date block (LSZB), a gradually withdrawing leisure network (EBAW), and local scheduled domestic traffic (NZNP). An **unweighted total observed-record count** would obscure this imbalance. In Phase6 collect the scheduled-population and observed-label denominators per airport/date/operator/route/horizon. In Phase7 report calendar-day clustered uncertainty and unseen directed-route Engine R performance separately; a model that wins on large HUBs may fail in small-region/rare-route strata, and no model can predict a route that had no valid features at its cutoff. No promise of “nearly accurate” global prediction is scientifically supported before the held-out study.

**What this checkpoint does NOT authorize:** replacing LSZB, EBAW or others by hand; altering the precommitted 1,013-row seed or original frame; declaring all 19 cities provider-supported; sending paid AeroDataBox requests; bypassing B0062, Phase5 FREEZE or Phase6 GO; rerunning successful migrations or P25 cleanup.
