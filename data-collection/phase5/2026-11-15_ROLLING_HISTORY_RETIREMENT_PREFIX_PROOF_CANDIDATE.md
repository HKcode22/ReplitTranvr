# Bounded controller history retirement — first verified prefix mechanism (candidate only)

Current deployed/reviewed controller has fixed original epoch, two protected 30-minute sessions, 96 shared ACKs, 450 requests/session, and six-hour read-history limit. Do not extend those arbitrarily; an older unknown or stopped worker cannot become invisible by resetting the timestamp.

This candidate adds one read-only method to the existing real GitHub snapshot reader (no new controller, database, workflow, or schedule). It computes a proposed history cut no later than a ten-minute overlap behind the observation clock, retains each lane's latest success while its existing cooldown is still active (including four-hour EARLY_DRAW), retains active-run history including old active runs, shrinks to at most 64 retired runs, and freshly reads exact job identity/required steps for **every removed run**, not just the latest. An older green wrapper with a gated required job cannot disappear behind newer green history. Only independently proved all-skipped schedules are inert. SAFE_PREFLIGHT_HOLD and MISSING_EVIDENCE remain distinguishable in counts/digest, not collection proof.

A future successor must re-read the old complete epoch and reproduce the exact prefix digest/counts, independently; late-visible rows, changed outcomes, context drift, incomplete history, old active custody, and stale proof cannot be ignored. No retired prefix is automatically forgotten: the new method is not yet wired into release/successor admission, does not compact the ACK ledger, and does not grant paid launch. Need verified predecessor artifact and exact root chain, live epoch rollover integration/long-run simulation, and actual DB attempt custody. GitHub can still have delayed visibility beyond any finite overlap; provider/DB admission remains authoritative. No provider call, role, migration, scheduling, or existing policy change.
