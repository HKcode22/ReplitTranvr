# Separate initial nonpaid source PREPARE before paid process arm

The existing Stage30 manual one-day PREPARE job previously required `V39_PHASE6_PROCESS_ARMED=1` despite its sole effect being the original atomic V0079/V0082 PREPARED query+partition rows. That would require paid arm before preparing actual source ledger evidence. The new exact private confirmation `ONE_PRIVATE_SIGNED_FUTURE_DAY_PREPARED_BEFORE_PAID_ARM` instead requires STOP flags, separate `V39_P5_PREPARE_PREARM_APPROVED=1`, and the unchanged exact signed packet bytes, real saved day/draw, independent freeze/rights/budget/manifest/role and actual Stage7/8/9 checks; no provider keys. Existing armed preparation mode remains separate. The Stage7 active signed database authorization and Stage9 seven-category budget checks remain mandatory; this does not permit prep with an absent real calendar, budget, rights or unreviewed freeze. No synthetic approval, no real execution, no automated per-day policy issuer or secret rotation. Test coverage verifies both modes and negations. Paid SOURCE child has its own unchanged arm and reservation gates. No production config or DB writes by this patch.
