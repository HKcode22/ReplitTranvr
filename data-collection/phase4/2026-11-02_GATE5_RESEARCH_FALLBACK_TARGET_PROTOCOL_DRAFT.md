## Why a fallback is needed

The reviewed original nine-window WSSS nonretained FIDS pilot and independent GH closeout succeeded 9/9, 18 measured REST units. Its original flight IDs/body and PRE were not saved, no per-flight first verified gate/runway scheduled T was established, and provider `departure.runwayTime` remains capable of being **estimated OR actual** per OpenAPI. Existing ADSB.lol data provides aircraft positions/traces, **not** official first scheduled gate-out or a per-record exact runway wheels-off label. More repeated GETs do not solve this semantic problem by themselves.

**Why status-only is not automatically valid:** A provider `Canceled` or `Diverted` status is evidence of what the provider reports at a time, not a complete population truth. A negative status is not proven merely because no alert arrived. Every positive/negative status must be materialized from a separately retained post-service original while within licensed content lifetime, with later corrections and conflicting sources classified separately. The post-service watermark and finalization policy must be frozen *before* the next cohort starts. Do not use later results to decide historical PRE membership or scheduling.
