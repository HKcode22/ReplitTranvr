## Research question
Previous source-original census on WSSS sampled two retained FIDS receipts / 31 departure source observations / nine AeroDataBox `IsOperator` records / zero independently certified canonical operating legs. The provider's original departure airport was missing in all 31; 22 were marketing codeshares, and no original actual runway/operating-flight linkage was established. The provider Flight Status GET `/flights/number/{number}/{dateLocal}?dateLocalRole=Departure` is officially Tier 2 and may expose explicit departure and destination airport ICAO, an aircraft registration/Mode S code and runwayTime, but those values are coverage-dependent and runwayTime **is not automatically actual**. Test one representative, previously selected WSSS IsOperator source-record against that endpoint.
