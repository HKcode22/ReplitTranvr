## Why this is needed

The November 2 WSSS **nonretained** 9/9 GETs (18 header-measured units) demonstrated reliable source-field acquisition and automated GitHub-only closeout, not a legal/retained flight-level PRE/POST cohort. A future `clean.fids_query_response` row can be linked by V0077 to a V0076 committed attempt, but that database linkage alone **does not prove** that it was requested using the same *reviewed* airport, local FIDS range, UTC service interval, leg expansion, role/cargo/private/cancelled filters, protocol revision, or query index as the frozen manifest.
