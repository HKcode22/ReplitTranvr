## The research question, without jargon

A webhook tells us **which flights sent us notifications**, not all flights in the departure population. A flight with no message must not silently disappear from the denominator. Before Phase 6, Gate 5 must validate a **provider-observable** flight population from AeroDataBox airport FIDS against webhook capture and outcomes, with a frozen time reference T and independent denominator counts. This is not a claim to enumerate every real-world flight. Phase 4 validates that later training examples are legitimate before we spend a month collecting them.

For every airport/time window/horizon, final required funnel is:
`FIDS primary members → confirmed physical operating legs → horizon eligibility → expected PRE rows → actual persisted PRE rows`, plus independent `captured-inside / captured-outside`, weather/other feature completeness and actual-only terminal outcome applicability/observability. The webhook is **parallel** to PRE eligibility. Any missing optional features may be `NULL` without erasing eligible PRE. Zero true denominator gives `rate=NULL, reason` rather than zero. Source known only after a cutoff may not backfill earlier existence.

Plan §6.0 defines `T` as **first verified provider-native schedule milestone**, NOT automatically FIDS `departure.scheduledTime` or an FAA gate-out/runway timestamp. At each actual flight's frozen T, `cutoff=T-24h`, `T-6h`, `T-90m`. `information_available_at<=cutoff` is the leakage guard. A revised/estimated `runwayTime` is not a certified actual wheels-off label. Marketing code-share rows cannot silently become independent operated legs. Retention rights are also a precondition of durable source storage.

## Nov 1 evening provider runway/ADSB.lol and parser-risk audit

**Research question:** Do 0 observed `departure.runwayTime.utc` fields in the real T−24h WSSS requests mean an AeroDataBox provider outage or a client parser failure, and can ADSB.lol substitute? Audited against live [official AeroDataBox RapidAPI OpenAPI v1.15.4.0](https://doc.aerodatabox.com/docs/openapi-rapidapi-v1.json), [vendor runway/ADS-B article](https://aerodatabox.com/flight-take-off-and-landing-time-runway-detection-based-on-ads-b-data-more-flights-and-status-updates/), [ADSB.lol API](https://api.adsb.lol/docs), [ADSB.lol historical archives](https://www.adsb.lol/docs/open-data/historical) and actual checked-in producer/inspection code. NO NEW PROVIDER GET; no paid workflow changes.

1. **Predeparture missing runway is expected, not proven failure.** T−24h WSSS pilot queried Nov1 16:30/17:00/17:30Z for NEXT DAY Nov2 17–18Z service. Source `departure.runwayTime` means *actual OR estimated* departure time on runway *if known*, per OpenAPI `FlightAirportMovementContract`. At T−24h, actual wheels-off is not yet physically observable. AeroDataBox's official ADS-B runway detection article states the inferred runway/time is *retroactive* and coverage varies by airport and receiver network. The 0/23 per retrieval is not a measured **post-flight** outcome coverage rate.
2. **Correct parser path for this source contract.** Current inspect-only script `server/lib/disruption/phase4Gate5OnePaidNonretainedSourceInspection_v39.ts` checks `f.departure.runwayTime.utc` and validates timestamp. With `withLeg=true` the documented FIDS `AirportFlightContract` exposes `departure` and `arrival` movement properties. No evidence of a runway-time key mapping error *at this path*. But the sample is aggregate-only and no source body survives: cannot rule out unexpected provider field variant or qualify an individual observed-vs-estimated value.
3. **Found genuine DIAGNOSTIC naming problems (non-blocking for timed GETs):** inspector reports `flightLegObjectPresent` from `f.leg`, but the official AirportFlightContract does **not define top-level `leg`**; `withLeg=true` expands `departure` and `arrival` instead. Therefore `0` top-level `leg` is not evidence that `withLeg` failed. Inspector separately reports `departure.airport.icao` presence, which official contract expressly says **can be absent when origin equals requested airport**; for WSSS departures, absence alone is expected and does not imply unknown origin or bad provider. Fix misleading metric names/contract tests after this frozen pilot; do not hot-patch paid source workflow in between its three time-critical horizons. Its `runwayTime` observation was at the correct path and remains genuine `0` on these preflight responses.
4. **Additional downstream identity software-policy risk:** existing `server/lib/disruption/fidsCensus_v3.ts::normalizeFidsRecord` correctly derives a *contextual origin* from a request for WSSS departures when `f.departure.airport.icao` is absent, BUT it deliberately keeps that record provisional (`context_departure_origin_unverified`) even if provider reports `IsOperator`. Official contract explicitly defines the requested airport as the departure airport when that field is omitted. This conservative rule may depress `confirmed_operating_leg` to zero in downstream population reports **even when provider data is sound**. Scientifically defensible next task is an offline source-contract and end-to-end provenance/ambiguity regression: establish that the exact query and requested-airport-primary role authenticate origin; retain ambiguous marketing/duplicate leg identity as distinct statuses, and verify full canonical chain against a licensed retained sample before promoting anything. **Do not blindly treat every `IsOperator` record as independently confirmed unique physical flight**.
5. **External source risk is distinct:** even **postflight**, official contract states `departure.runwayTime` is **actual OR estimated**, not an item-level actuality certificate; `departure.revisedTime` also actual OR estimated, and the coarse `quality` array `Basic/Live/Approximate` does not prove per-field actualness. Thus labels must not auto-promote from a non-null value. Check source-native actual-only/provenance per record or explicitly choose a versioned separate label; this is blocking for original exact wheels-off target. AeroDataBox publishes a **free-tier** airport feed health endpoint `GET /health/services/airports/{icao}/feeds` for `FlightSchedules/FlightLiveUpdates/AdsbUpdates`; a future **free-only** capacity check could identify whether current WSSS ADS-B coverage is available without a speculative paid postflight request. Availability reports service health, **not actual timestamp truth**.
6. **ADSB.lol is a potential independent position/history supplement, not current collection's runway output.** Current Nov2 paid runner calls ONLY AeroDataBox WSSS FIDS. ADSB.lol's official docs describe live aircraft positions and ODbL-licensed historical daily aircraft traces with `lat/lon`, altitude, speed, track and timestamps; **it does not provide a provider-native scheduled gate/runway T or certify exact actual wheels-off per operated flight** from those observables alone. Physical takeoff may be inferable with uncertainty and coverage exclusions, properly labelled `ADSB_INFERRED_TAKEOFF_PROXY`, not actual-only wheels-off unless independently calibrated and accepted as a versioned alternative endpoint. Verify ADSB.lol license obligations, accessibility, service reliability, matching/callsign/hex/aircraft swap before operational use; do not install a new provider in Phase4 by stealth.
7. **Long-term ML consequence:** Training precise wheels-off delay needs a known schedule milestone, a genuinely actual takeoff timestamp **and per-flight matching**; absence creates missing targets, not `0` delay. If label missingness varies by airport/airline/receiver coverage it induces selection bias. Evaluate label-observability rates by stratum and compare captured/noncaptured, do not compute accuracy only on a distorted subset without a caveat. Alternate cancellation/diversion/arrival tasks may remain viable if each has independent verified outcome sources, but model target must be frozen/reported separately. Source/content retention still subject to default 7-day terms, current account rights and Nov7 notice; see issue #300.
8. **Impact on CURRENT paid campaign:** No evidence of actual HTTP failure; real T−24h succeeded 3/3 and measured six units. The **future T−6h and T−90m are still PRE-departure**, so expecting actual takeoff then is also a timing mistake. Do not launch an unreviewed late catch-up or change the nine-slot manifest. The missing true runway label can only be investigated from appropriate **postflight**, legally accessible data and provenance after the service window, not retroactive rewriting of pre-departure sources. Gate5 remains source-only operational success / scientific HOLD.
