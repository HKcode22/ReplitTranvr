- [Current official Terms of Use](https://aerodatabox.com/terms), last updated October 11, 2026, Arts 5.5 and 5.6: default content caching limit is **7 consecutive calendar days**, subject to authenticated response `Cache-Control: max-age`, actual plan exceptions, statutory exceptions, and certain narrow Article 5.8 categories. The permitted purpose can require **earlier deletion**; the seven-day calculation is only an upper-bound screening interval, **not automatic authorization to store for seven days**.
- Terms define **Contents** broadly, including extracts, copies, selected/reformatted fields and per-flight records. A normalized per-flight row is not automatically a genuine non-reconstructable **Derived Work**; the Derived Work definition requires independent non-trivial work across observations or with substantial independent sources plus non-reconstruction. Article 5.6 includes exceptions for qualified Derived Works, but it is not a blanket exemption for row-normalization.
- The terms prohibit unlicensed permanent copies; Art 5.8 separately permits only specific limited categories and uses, subject to strict anti-pooling/repurposing conditions. An ordinary cohort warehouse and model training **cannot simply be declared an incidental log, business record or personalized user record** to bypass Art 5.5.
- [Official pricing](https://aerodatabox.com/pricing) distinguishes standard and extended cache rights; the actual **RapidAPI** subscription tier and any special agreement have **not** been independently authenticated in these Phase 4 checkpoints. In particular, the announcement lists RapidAPI **Mega** as an extended-caching option; do not assume the user's plan is Mega.
- The original source physics/target issue remains unresolved: official OpenAPI v1.15.4.0 generic `scheduledTime` is not a verified first native selected T; `revisedTime` and `runwayTime` may be actual OR estimated; `quality=Live` is not a per-value actual-only certificate.
