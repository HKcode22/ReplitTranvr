## 5. Proposed architecture and change process
Layer 1 immutable study protocol and data contract; layer 2 durable acquisition/task ledger and restricted data workers; layer 3 independently released orchestration, scheduling and diagnostics; layer 4 deployment and operator control. Data lineage identifies actual producer version and original signed packet; a consumer's current executable identity is distinct, authorized only by reviewed compatibility manifest. Use existing DB transaction/idempotency and budget reservations rather than parallel flag copies. Keep physical billable attempt fencing, immutable native T and cutoff, authentic POST/independent evidence and provider retention. Prefer modular release units and contract tests, not microservices for their own sake. Maintain a durable owner-controlled versioned backup of noncredential policy material, encrypted/access controlled; keys/credentials stay separately in supported secret storage. No secrets in public GitHub, logs or generated docs.

Change classification:
A operational code-only (same scientific data contract, no rights/cap change): normal reviewed implementation change + regression, versioned deploy and rollback. B engineering changes affecting timing/coverage/measurement: prospective change with scientific comparability and missingness review before deploy. C study design/legal/spend change: independent explicit prospective amendment, version cohort and denominators, no retroactive change; owner decision. No blanket unfreeze. Existing Phase6 is priority; conduct inventory and tests on isolated branch with synthetic/disposable data, no paid calls, no production DB changes, no extra launch gates. Only separately approved urgent repair may be considered for coordinated production deployment. Once approved, staged cutover, actual same-workload no-provider proof, inspect effects, rollback if not safe; no resetting epochs to hide uncertainty.
