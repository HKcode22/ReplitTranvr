## A. Why this amendment exists

P2G06 WSSS completed approximately the full two-hour live exposure and persisted 36 AeroDataBox webhook payloads. A read-only reconstruction verified:

- 36/36 payload SHA-256 values;
- 219 flight items;
- 219 explicit `deliveryAttempt.costCredits`;
- zero received payloads where `costCredits` disagreed with item count;
- provider external balance delta of 220 credits;
- exact external-minus-internal gap of 1 credit.

AeroDataBox documents credit-based Flight Alert billing as one credit per flight item **when the alert is sent**, not when successful delivery is confirmed. A failed/undelivered send can therefore consume credits even with retries disabled.

Provider reference:
https://aerodatabox.com/flight-alert-api-2026/

P2G06 remains historical `failed/MISMATCH`. It is not retroactively converted to PASS.

## C. Prospective reconciliation v2

### C.1 Authoritative denominator

For Stage-1/2 anchor yield metrics:

`C_external = balance_before - settled_balance_after`

remains the authoritative cost denominator.

The received webhook ledger remains an independent delivery/accounting diagnostic.

### C.2 Exact match

If:

`C_external == C_internal`

and no received payload has a cost/item contradiction, reconciliation is `MATCH`.

### C.3 Bounded delivery gap

For **anchor probes only**, a run may be classified `DELIVERY_GAP` and remain scoreable only when all are true:

1. settled external spend exists and is nonnegative;
2. internal received credits are nonnegative;
3. `C_external - C_internal == 1` at most;
4. `C_internal / C_external >= 0.99`;
5. internal never exceeds external;
6. zero received payloads have `costCredits != notification_items`;
7. provider settlement resolves;
8. the run is not duration-censored;
9. all other callback/runtime/cleanup gates pass.

Why the one-item bound is conservative:

- AeroDataBox bills in one-credit-per-flight-item units.
- A capacity-valid two-hour Stage-1 probe must receive at least 120 rows under the existing 60 rows/hour capacity gate.
- One missing billed item at 120 received rows is <0.84% incompleteness.
- The rule is prospective and hash-frozen before the next paid attempt.

### C.4 Metric treatment under DELIVERY_GAP

Never inflate observed scientific counts.

- rows/hour uses received rows only;
- confirmed unique lower uses observed confirmed unique flights only;
- tail-chain links use observed links only;
- all per-credit yield denominators use external billed spend;
- unique-flight upper bound is widened by at most the missing billed-item count.

This makes the observed nominal/lower metrics conservative.

### C.5 Hard failures remain hard failures

The following are not tolerated:

- internal > external;
- gap > 1 credit;
- delivery completeness < 99%;
- any received `costCredits != item count`;
- unresolved settlement;
- provider/account uncertainty;
- foreign billable subscription;
- failed callback/raw persistence that violates other safety gates;
- duration-censored scientific run.

Safety smoke/canary continues to require exact reconciliation.

## D. Durable evidence before cleanup

Before temporary provider/runtime evidence can be cleaned, every anchor probe must persist append-only aggregate reconciliation evidence containing:

- probe id, stage, ICAO, runtime session UUID;
- evidence status;
- external spend;
- internal received credits;
- delivery gap;
- delivery completeness;
- delivery count;
- notification items;
- explicit-cost vs fallback counts;
- cost/item disagreement count;
- callback requests/success/failure counters;
- settlement reads;
- maximum observed unsettled gap;
- window start/end;
- duration-censored state;
- stop reason.

Raw provider payloads and provider subscription IDs do not belong in this durable table.

`migrations/0058_phase2g_reconciliation_evidence.sql`

## E. Callback latency hardening

The prepaid callback previously inserted every flight item with one SQL round-trip per item.

The corrected path bulk-inserts all items in a webhook payload in one statement. This reduces callback response latency while preserving the rule that raw blob persistence and normalized runtime persistence complete before HTTP 2xx.

This directly reduces the risk of a provider delivery timeout on large airport payloads.

## G. Stage 2 becomes conditional

The compact design does not automatically require five additional four-hour Stage-2 confirmations.

Trigger confirmation only if one of the frozen conditions occurs:

1. fewer than five valid compact Stage-1 candidates;
2. a candidate fails the 60 rows/hour capacity gate;
3. final-five membership is not invariant under recorded identity bounds;
4. WSSS and OMAA are both invalid as reference;
5. fifth-versus-sixth membership is not robust.

If no trigger occurs, report the result as:

**compact Stage-1-selected anchor pool**

not as the original V3.9 Stage-2-confirmed pool.
