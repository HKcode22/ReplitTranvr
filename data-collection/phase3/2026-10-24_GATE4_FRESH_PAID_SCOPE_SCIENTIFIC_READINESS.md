## Quantitative interpretation (not permission to certify reliability)

- **Feasibility:** at least one genuine owned-ID Alert received with public positive acknowledgment, exact raw wire SHA, source/time/attribution proof, followed by exact independent STOP/zero-active and stable three-sample credit settlement. This is `QUALIFIED_LIVE_ALERT_FEASIBILITY_OBSERVED` only. Callback path-secret and subscription ID inside JSON are **claims** unless the provider attempt has independent attestation; do not portray these as cryptographic vendor signatures.
- **No observation:** if neither independently known provider SEND attempt nor billed credits nor genuine original appear after a short watch, the result is **inconclusive** rather than 0% delivery success. If paid credits/independently known SENDs are positive yet genuine originals are zero, call it **possible delivery/ingestion gap**, not proof of which side lost the message.
- **Reliability rate:** must know the number of independent genuine provider SEND attempts and their independently verified receipt outcomes, the sampling frame, duplicates and censoring. Do **not divide original rows by 46 billed credits**; different units, and 46 may be from one or more bundled notifications.
- An **illustrative** 95% *two-sided exact Clopper–Pearson* lower bound with `n` independent Bernoulli sender trials and `n` successes is `0.025^(1/n)`. For only one independently observed provider notification and one success, lower bound = **2.5%**, upper bound 100% (the raw sample fraction 1/1 cannot establish production reliability). If **36/36** independent, representative notification trials succeed, lower bound is **about 90.26%**. Sender independence and representative random sampling are not currently established. This calculation **must not be run on synthetic test or billed credits**.
