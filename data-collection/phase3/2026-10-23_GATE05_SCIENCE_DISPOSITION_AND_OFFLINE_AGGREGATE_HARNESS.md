## Verified external contracts and the exact scientific obstacle

The [official AeroDataBox OpenAPI version 1.15.4.0](https://doc.aerodatabox.com/docs/openapi-rapidapi-v1.json), schemas `AirportFlightContract` and `FlightAirportMovementContract`, defines:
- `departure.scheduledTime` as scheduled arrival/departure time, **not unambiguously scheduled wheels-off**.
- `departure.revisedTime` as actual/**estimated** and either gate-side or runway-side, depending on whether a distinct `runwayTime` is present.
- `departure.runwayTime` as actual/**estimated** takeoff/landing time when known; mere presence (even if quality `Live`) does **not** identify an observed actual takeoff timestamp.
- `quality` as `Basic`, `Live`, `Approximate`: metadata about movement quality, **not a per-field `isActual` assertion**.
- The `departure.airport` field may be **omitted** when it represents the currently requested airport; absence should not be counted as a known opposite-airport movement. Feed context is not proof of canonical operating-leg identity.

The [FAA ASPM taxi-time variable definitions](https://www.aspm.faa.gov/aspmhelp/index/ASPM_Taxi_Times__Definitions_of_Variables.html) and [FAA individual-flight definitions](https://www.aspm.faa.gov/aspmhelp/index/ASPM__Individual_Flights__Definitions_of_Variables.html) explicitly separate scheduled gate-out, estimated scheduled wheels-off, actual gate-out and actual wheels-off. The chosen target `actual_wheels_off - scheduled_wheels_off` **must not** be constructed by blindly computing `runwayTime - scheduledTime` without milestone semantics. An honest alternate outcome could be **actual wheels-off relative to published scheduled departure**, but that is a *different operational estimand*, must be explicitly named and approved, and still needs validated actual wheels-off; it is not a substitute for proving the originally requested scheduled-runway reference. Missing or estimated outcomes should be classified missing/censored with denominator and missingness accounting, not treated as zero delay.
