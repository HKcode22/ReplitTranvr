## Gate 0.5: identify our software/measurement shortcoming versus external data semantics

**Observed fact:** FIDS two real 30-record responses were returned. The paid operator [`scripts/v39_gate05_scoped_fids_feasibility_operator_v39.ts`](../../scripts/v39_gate05_scoped_fids_feasibility_operator_v39.ts) intentionally serialized only: a `response` status, `departures` count, and **top-level** `departureFieldNames` for each `withLocation` variation. It did **not** retain inner `departure.scheduledTime`, `revisedTime`, `runwayTime`, which field was actual vs estimated, actual first-seen/as-of timestamps, canonical flight-ID overlaps, or sample-level source provenance. This was a **local measurement-plan/instrumentation omission**, NOT evidence that AeroDataBox returned zero runway data or failed to return FIDS. It also respected an intentional aggregate-only/nonraw retention design; do not label it payload corruption or retroactively infer values.

**Separate unresolved provider limitation:** the [official AeroDataBox OpenAPI](https://doc.aerodatabox.com/docs/openapi-rapidapi-v1.json) permits departure `runwayTime` to be **actual or estimated**. `scheduledTime` is scheduled departure but not definitively separate scheduled wheels-off. `quality=Live` is not a field-level actual-wheels-off proof. Thus even retaining all nested fields cannot **guarantee** our exact target `actual wheels_off minus scheduled wheels_off` without authoritative event semantics (provider clarification, state transitions and independent milestone source). The desired label is **NOT_PROVEN**, not a reported physical-flight loss or failed GET.

**Existing tested improvement:** merged [PR #112](https://github.com/HKcode22/ReplitTranvr/pull/112) includes the no-provider in-memory `server/lib/disruption/gate05FidsAggregateEvidence_v39.ts` with counts for nested scheduled/revised/runway presence, UTC window membership, `withLocation` comparison and explicitly unknown actuality; fixes false-zero for omitted departure airport under provider-documented requested-airport context *as provisional* (not certified identity). It does **not** reconstruct the historical paid responses and is **not yet a demonstrated production pilot label extractor**.

**Decision before any new paid FIDS test:**
1. Source audit, no provider spend: compare FIDS JSON contracts to required **actual wheels-off**, **scheduled wheels-off** and forecast-time cutoff `T`. Seek provider support or second licensed actual-off-block/airborne/wheels-off reference as needed; review existing legitimately retained data, not expired original payloads.
2. Make an *offline* sample/contract probe harness that accepts provider responses in memory, returns minimal permitted **aggregate counts** of actuality evidence, missingness, identity overlap, `T` availability and delay label validity, without writing raw identifiers, times, keys or unsanctioned provider data to GitHub.
3. Only if timestamp source semantics can be demonstrated and fresh measurements are genuinely required: new **separately approved**, bounded, exact-source and STOP-gated pilot designed to collect the necessary provenance aggregates. Do **not** reuse expired WSSS12 paid permit or repeat the same two blind queries. If only a scheduled-gate baseline is available, define that as an explicitly different target for approval, never silently redefine original runway-delay outcome.
4. Independently gate Phase 4 **paid** label acquisition on a named label source, actual-vs-estimated decision, as-of timing `T`, population/codeshare identity, storage/retention rights and a predeclared missingness protocol. Phase4 **offline** code/design can proceed immediately.

**6+6 meaning:** the [source-reviewed Stage1 watchdog state machine](../../scripts/v39_phase2g_stage1_watchdog_6plus6_policy_v39.ts) permits **6 primary + 6 conditional backup health observations**, bounded by a 180s overall envelope and episode controls; it is **not twelve provider redelivery attempts**. The P25 owner reported `callback_watchdog_triggered=false`, `callback_consecutive_failures_at_exit=0`, 58 observed 2xx, no proven incoming callback-processing failures. **A healthy receiver and no sustained outage do not prove the provider attempted-to-send denominator or account for the external two units.** An unobserved sender attempt, edge/host transport issue, ACK timing, billing aggregation/credit posting, or a provider payload/policy variant are hypotheses only. The P25 exact sender-side original-attempt ledger was **not independently available** (`callback_watchdog_source_proof_implemented=false`). Do not claim a YSSY application callback 422 like Oct25 NEXT3; they are distinct incidents and the YSSY 58 observed calls succeeded.

**Historical retention:** the P25 58 old raw-provider objects reached TTL and were subsequently **exactly deleted/tombstoned** on Oct22 per explicit owner-approved scoped retention, with independently audited 58/58 physical absence in configured dedicated bucket (issue #38 comments 6336188215, 6336372883).
