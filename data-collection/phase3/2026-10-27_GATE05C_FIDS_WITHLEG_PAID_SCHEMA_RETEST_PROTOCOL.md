## Exact known outcome and why the next test is different

Historical Gate05 WSSS12 and Gate05b actual billable REST retrievals **already passed technical transport/billing**. On Oct27 Gate05b returned 15 departure objects with `withLocation=false` and another 15 with `withLocation=true`, 4 units billed, zero Alert credits; strict `T`-only UTC validation silently rejected vendor-published space-delimited `DateTimeContract.utc` examples. [Merged PR #180](https://github.com/HKcode22/ReplitTranvr/pull/180) repaired this parser and tested it offline. Neither historical response retained flight-level JSON; their nested field values are **unrecoverable**. A repeat of the same location toggle would be redundant.

**NEW diagnostic question:** does the same WSSS 20-minute post-flight window return nonempty UTC schedule/revised/runway times and a usable departure/movement branch with `withLeg=false` versus `withLeg=true`? The [AeroDataBox official FIDS contract](https://doc.aerodatabox.com/docs/openapi-rapidapi-v1.json) distinguishes the `movement` branch from `departure`/`arrival` depending on `withLeg`. [Vendor withLeg explanation](https://aerodatabox.com/fids-with-extended-information-flight-time-and-distance/) says the response structure changes. This A/B retest changes **one relevant schema dimension** while keeping location false; its field-shape histogram can distinguish *missing*, *present and parseable SPACE UTC*, *present and parseable T UTC*, and *present but invalid UTC* without persisting raw source records.

## Explicit pass classes and science boundary

- **Paid transport/accounting measured**: two physical FIDS GETs 200 with parseable departures arrays, quota delta exactly 4, Alert credit delta 0, no retries. This is all the script's success status certifies.
- **Contract diagnosis**: per variant, count `movement` vs `departure`, nonempty UTC and valid UTC values for each scheduled/revised/runway time, format buckets (missing/space/T/invalid), `lastUpdatedUtc` format presence if supplied, codeshare operator/marketing and provisional identity counts. Expected source branch is `movement` for withLeg=false and `departure` for true; any mismatch is visible. Even observed empty/malformed fields do not prove the *entire* provider never has runway data.
- **Original runway-delay label remains NOT PROVEN** even if all fields appear. Official [`FlightAirportMovementContract`](https://doc.aerodatabox.com/docs/openapi-rapidapi-v1.json) says `runwayTime` is **actual/estimated**, and `scheduledTime` is departure schedule, not independently scheduled wheels-off. The [FAA definitions](https://www.aspm.faa.gov/aspmhelp/index/ASPM__Individual_Flights__Definitions_of_Variables.html) distinguish these delay constructs. A historical postflight lookup cannot prove what predictions saw before departure (`T`). A new label study needs actual-event provenance and independently sourced scheduled-runway reference, or owner approval of a differently named proxy.
