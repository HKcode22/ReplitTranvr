## Exact GO / NO-GO test order
1. **Before window selection:** freeze a single exact source SHA and 4/4 green tests; independently verify signed auth schema, current provider tier/plan/retention terms; declare zero paid GETs until all other steps pass.
2. **Non-paid end-to-end dry run:** use disposable PG16 with fake signed authorization, real restricted test logins, two exact prepared queries, one-use permit, STOP function, controlled faults, and compare reserved units vs durable terminal receipt. No AeroDataBox key or production database credentials in this rehearsal.
3. **Pre-run credential session:** one-session mint/verify unique restricted safety, commission, activation, dispatch identities. Never save plaintext passwords in volatile workspace for later retrieval; use approved encrypted secret mechanisms, independently confirm expiry+auth/roles.
4. **Exact signed and prepared live scope:** freshly sign only after readiness and source frozen; review public SHA/pin and provider rights; register one unused exact permit then consume to PREPARED with verified zero attempts; avoid re-executing any step after partial COMMIT without read-only readback.
5. **Independent protected job:** encrypt signed input and pins, launch manual exact `main`/scope/SHA STOP job, verify that its runtime session is authenticated restricted safety role, check its in-progress step and **separately test error/incident delivery and human acknowledgment**.
6. **Activation GO gate:** only after independent proof above, activate exact PREPARED scope as distinct activation-only identity **within signed window**; read back ACTIVE, caps, query hashes and zero attempts. No arbitrary owner table UPDATE or broad login for dispatcher.
7. **Capped GET:** two signed FIDS queries OFF/ON; at most 6 physical GET attempts reserved 2 units each across both. Abort on any job-status mismatch, expired time, privilege problem or unknown provider plan; don't refund ambiguous reservations.
8. **Terminal reconciliation:** independent STOP readback, counts from DB attempt ledger vs provider's actual API unit delta, expired job/alerts, missingness statistics, retention cleanup. Do not claim scientific GO from two responses alone.
