# Travnr SEO Backlink Plan

This file lists ethical, off-site link-building ideas to help Travnr rank
for **AI travel concierge / AI travel planner / AI travel agent** queries.

> **Important:** Backlinks cannot be created from inside this codebase.
> Every item below requires human outreach, account creation, or
> publishing on third-party sites. Track outreach in a separate doc or
> spreadsheet — do not check off items here based on code changes.

## Why backlinks matter
Google still uses high-quality inbound links as a strong ranking signal,
especially for a brand new domain like `travnr.com`. A handful of
relevant, trustworthy links will move the needle far more than dozens of
spammy directory submissions.

## 1. Startup & product directories (low effort, decent authority)
- **Product Hunt** — launch when the AI call flow + booking is solid
  enough to handle a traffic spike. Coordinate with hunters in advance.
- **BetaList / Betapage** — submit while still in beta to attract early
  adopters and a free dofollow link.
- **AlternativeTo** — create an entry positioning Travnr as an
  "AI alternative" to traditional OTAs and concierge agencies.
- **There's An AI For That**, **Futurepedia**, **Future Tools**,
  **AI Tool Hunt**, **Toolify** — high-traffic AI tool aggregators.
  Submit once with consistent copy ("AI travel concierge / AI travel
  planner") and a clean screenshot.
- **G2 / Capterra / GetApp** — only worthwhile once there are real
  users willing to leave honest reviews; do not seed fake reviews.

## 2. Travel-tech & niche directories
- **Skift / PhocusWire** startup roundups (pitch via their tip lines).
- **Travel Massive** community profile.
- **Travolution** new-product mentions.
- Niche AI-travel listicles ("best AI trip planners 2026") — reach out
  to authors with a short, honest pitch and a screenshot.

## 3. University & accelerator pages (high authority, .edu)
- **WashU / Skandalaris Center** founder profile or program alumni
  page (the founder's affiliation per the brief).
- Any other accelerator, incubator, or university entrepreneurship
  program Travnr has been part of — these almost always link back to
  member companies.
- Hackathon / pitch-competition recap pages where Travnr was mentioned.

## 4. Founder presence (LinkedIn, X, personal site)
- Founder LinkedIn posts about building Travnr, the AI call flow, and
  beta lessons learned, each linking to `https://travnr.com` and the
  blog post or feature it discusses.
- Founder X/Threads/Bluesky bio link + occasional product threads.
- Founder personal site / portfolio listing Travnr as a current project.
- Speaking gigs, podcast appearances, and meetup talks — every host
  page is a potential backlink.

## 5. Guest posts & content collaborations
- Guest posts on travel blogs, productivity blogs, and AI-tool blogs
  about "how I use AI to plan trips," "what an AI travel concierge
  actually does," etc. Always include an honest author bio with a link
  to Travnr.
- Co-marketing posts with complementary tools (calendar apps, expense
  trackers, travel insurance) where each side links to the other.
- Podcast interviews focused on AI products or travel-tech.

## 6. Press & PR (when there's a real story)
- Press releases via PRWeb / EIN Presswire only when there's something
  genuinely newsworthy (funding, public launch, milestone). Avoid
  press-release spam.
- Reach out to local Missouri / St. Louis tech journalists — local
  press tends to cover homegrown founders.
- HARO / Qwoted / Help A B2B Writer responses on travel-tech and
  AI-product topics.

## 7. Beta-tester referral & word of mouth
- A simple "share Travnr" link in the post-call email or proposal
  (existing transactional email) — once feature work allows. This is a
  product change, not an outreach task; tracked here for completeness.
- Encourage early users to mention Travnr in their own travel
  newsletters, Slack groups, or Discord communities.

## What NOT to do
- **No paid link networks**, PBNs, or "guaranteed dofollow" services.
  These get the domain penalized.
- **No fake reviews or fake testimonials.** They violate the policies
  of every directory above and erode user trust.
- **No keyword-stuffed anchor text.** Use natural anchors like
  "Travnr" or "AI travel concierge", not "best ai travel planner cheap
  flights deals".
- **No mass directory submissions** to low-quality sites — they're
  ignored at best, harmful at worst.

## Tracking
Keep a separate sheet (Notion / Airtable / Google Sheets) with:
`source · target URL · contact · date pitched · status · live URL · DR/DA`.
Re-evaluate the list quarterly and prune dead links.
